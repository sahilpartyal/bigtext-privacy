02 What the App Does

Bigtext Board displays the text you type across the full screen of your device, so it can be read from a distance — at an airport pickup, on a shop counter, at an event, or during a presentation.

The app works completely offline. There is no account, no sign-up, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

The free version is supported by advertising served through Google AdMob, and includes a limited feature set:

Upgrading to Bigtext Pro removes all advertising. How advertising data is handled is described in our Privacy Policy.

05 Bigtext Pro Plans

Bigtext Pro unlocks all 12 fonts, all 15 colours, your own logo on the display, 50 saved messages, and removes every ad. It is available in three forms:

Monthly

Billed every month. Renews automatically until you cancel.

Yearly

Billed once a year at a lower effective rate. Renews automatically until you cancel.

Lifetime

A single one-time payment. Does not renew and is never charged again.

The exact price and billing period are always shown on the purchase screen inside the app, in your own currency, before you confirm anything. We never display a price that has not come from the store itself.

06 Automatic Renewal & Cancelling

Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period, and your payment method is charged at the same price, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

You can cancel at any time and keep Pro features until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Android — Google Play Store app > your profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Bigtext Board > Cancel subscription
iOS — Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Bigtext Board > Cancel subscription
Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. You must cancel through the store as described above.
